## ■ Featured Projects

### ■ Project 01 — Voice Insight IQ

**AI-Powered Multi-Platform E-Commerce Comparison & Recommendation System**

Voice Insight IQ helps users discover and compare products across multiple e-commerce platforms. It focuses on comparing **prices, ratings, reviews, offers, and product information** to help users make better purchasing decisions.

**Key Features:**

* 🔎 Multi-platform product search
* 💰 Price comparison
* ⭐ Ratings & review comparison
* 🤖 AI-powered recommendations
* 🎙️ Voice-based product search
* 🛒 Direct product purchase links
* 📊 Product comparison

**Tech Stack:**

`Python` `FastAPI` `SQL` `AI` `REST APIs` `Data Processing`

---

### ■ Project 02 — MyGuru Rural

**Low-Data & Accessible EdTech Platform**

MyGuru Rural is an educational platform designed to make learning more accessible for students, particularly in areas with limited internet connectivity.

The platform focuses on **offline/low-data learning, regional language support, and accessible educational content**.

**Key Features:**

* 📚 Educational resources
* 📱 Mobile-first experience
* 🌐 Regional language support
* 📶 Low-data learning
* 💾 Offline-friendly learning
* 🎯 Student-focused interface

**Tech Stack:**

`Figma` `Supabase` `UI/UX` `Mobile Design`

---

### ■ Project 03 — CampusHUB

**All-in-One Student Communication & Opportunity Platform**

CampusHUB is a college-focused platform designed to help students easily access important college information while discovering academic and career opportunities.

Students can use the platform to stay updated with **college details, assignments, internships, job opportunities, and hackathons** in one place.

**Key Features:**

* 🏫 College information
* 📝 Assignment updates
* 📢 Student communication
* 💼 Internship opportunities
* 💻 Job opportunities
* 🏆 Hackathon discovery
* 🔍 Opportunity search

**Tech Stack:**

`HTML` `CSS` `JavaScript` `UI/UX` `Database`
